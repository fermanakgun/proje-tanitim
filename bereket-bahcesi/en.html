About

Bountiful Grove is a calm fruit-matching puzzle you can play at your own pace.

Match apples, pears, grapes, lemons, plums and strawberries in threes and watch your orchard grow from spring to winter. There are no lives, no waiting timers and no time limits; lose a level and try again right away.

Every level is generated from its number and play-tested by a computer player before you see it. Difficulty rises gently, easy “breather” levels come often, and the game never becomes impossible.

Large tiles, high contrast and Dynamic Type support keep it comfortable for everyone, and each fruit has its own color and its own shape. It works fully offline and collects no personal data.

Key features

  • No lives, no waiting, no time limits; no ads and no in-app purchases
  • Endless levels generated from their number and play-tested in advance
  • Heart, flower, leaf and ring shaped boards
  • Bee, Rain Cloud and Golden Seed helpers, with stronger effects when combined
  • Seasons that change every 25 levels: spring, summer, autumn, winter
  • Color-blind friendly: every fruit has its own color and shape
  • Soft animations with no sudden flashes; sound, music and haptics toggle separately
  • Fully offline; progress is saved on device
