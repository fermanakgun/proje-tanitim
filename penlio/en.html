About

Penlio is a handwritten notebook and PDF notes app for iPad and iPhone, made for students.

Organise your notebooks in folders with colourful covers, take handwritten class notes, and import PDFs to write on them. It is completely free: no ads, no subscription, no account and no locked features. It collects no data. If you like, you can leave an optional in-app tip (payment is handled by Apple).

Features

  • Folder and notebook library; 60+ covers, a cover studio and your own cover designs
  • Page templates: plain, lined, grid, dotted, Cornell; paged or infinite page
  • Pen presets, fountain pen, highlighter, eraser
  • Lasso selection: move, resize, recolour; magnifier
  • Import PDFs and write on them; export as PDF or images
  • Document scanning, photos and text boxes; shape correction
  • Flashcards for studying
  • Planner, class schedule and widget
  • Stickers, washi tape and sticky notes
  • Table of contents, tabs, bookmarks and Face ID notebook lock
  • Audio recording: tap your writing to replay the sound
  • On-device AI summaries (Apple Foundation Models); optionally connect your own OpenAI/Claude key: the key stays in the Keychain and requests go straight from your device to the provider, with no server in between
  • Two notebooks side by side, presentation mode and laser pointer
  • On-device search in your handwriting; iCloud sync (iPhone ↔ iPad, only in your own private iCloud)
  • 22 languages; in-app language picker
  • 8 alternate app icons
  • Free, no ads, no subscription, no account
