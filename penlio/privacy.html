Penlio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17 · Türkçe

Personal data

Penlio does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account or sign-up.

Where your notes are stored

Your notes, notebooks, PDFs and the search index are stored only on your device and in your own iCloud account (Apple’s private CloudKit database). The developer cannot access them.

Handwriting recognition and search

Handwriting recognition and search happen on your device; your content is not sent to any server.

Ads, analytics and tracking

The app has no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo access is requested only when you choose to add a photo to a page.

Tip payments

Optional in-app tip payments are processed entirely by Apple; no payment information reaches the developer.
