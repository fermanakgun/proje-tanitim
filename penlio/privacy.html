Penlio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Türkçe

Personal data

Penlio does not collect, store or share any personal data (App Store label: “Data Not Collected”). No account or sign-up is needed. The developer has no server, account system, ads, analytics or tracking.

Where are my notes stored?

Your notes, notebooks, PDFs, audio recordings and search index are stored only on your device and in your own iCloud account (Apple’s private CloudKit database). The developer cannot access this data.

Handwriting recognition and search

Handwriting recognition and search run on your device (Apple Vision); your content is not sent to any server.

Optional AI summaries

By default, AI features run on your device (Apple Foundation Models) and data never leaves it. Only if you add YOUR OWN OpenAI or Anthropic Claude API key under Settings > AI, the page text you select is sent DIRECTLY from your device to that provider, after you confirm a warning shown before every send. The developer does not see or store this data, and there is no server in between. This transfer is governed by the provider’s own privacy policy: the OpenAI and Anthropic privacy policies. Your API key is stored only in your device’s Keychain and is not synced; deleting the key stops the transfer.

Permissions

Microphone: only for audio recording; recordings stay on your device and in your iCloud. Camera: to add photos to a page and to scan documents. Face ID: for notebook lock; biometric data never reaches the app, iOS performs the check. Photo picker: only hands the app the images you choose. These permissions are requested only when you use the related feature.

Sharing

Notebook export and AirDrop are file shares that you start yourself; you decide who receives the file and where it goes. The developer does not receive these files.

Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.

Tip payments

Optional in-app tips are processed entirely by Apple; no personal data or payment details reach the developer.
