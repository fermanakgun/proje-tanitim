Türkçe

How does iCloud sync work?

If iCloud is enabled for Penlio on devices signed in with the same Apple ID, your notebooks sync automatically. If they do not, check that the device is online, you are signed in to iCloud and your iCloud storage is not full.

How do I import a PDF?

Use the import option in the library and choose a PDF; it becomes a notebook you can write on with the pen. You can then export and share it as a PDF or images.

How do I search my handwriting?

Type the word in the search field; search runs on your device, over your handwriting.

Do I have to tip?

No. Penlio is completely free and tips are optional. To request a refund for a tip, use Apple’s refund page (reportaproblem.apple.com); Apple handles the payment.

Where is my data?

Only on your device and in your own iCloud account; the developer cannot access it. See the privacy policy.
