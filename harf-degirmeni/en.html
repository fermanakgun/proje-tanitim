About

Letter Mill (Harf Değirmeni) is a calm Turkish word puzzle: swipe across the letters on the millstone to make words, with no timer and no penalties.

Swipe across the letters on the millstone to join them; the words you find fill the small crossword above. Valid words that are not in the puzzle are collected in your My Bonus Words list.

There is no timer, no lives, no penalties and no waiting; a wrong word costs you nothing. Hints and Shuffle are always available and unlimited. The 300 levels are grouped into fifteen mills of 20 levels each, and no word appears in two different levels.

Designed to be easy on the eyes, with large letters, big buttons and high contrast. The text size is adjustable; light and dark appearance, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion are supported. Calm background music (can be turned off) and wooden-sounding effects accompany play. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no tracking; it works fully offline.
