About

Diamond Wardrobe is a free game where you solve maths questions to earn diamonds and dress up your character.

Kids solve maths questions, earn diamonds with correct answers, try items on their character in the boutique fitting room and buy the ones they like. They dress a girl or boy chibi character, save outfits, take photos with a scene and a pet, and keep them in an album.

Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division questions are read aloud, and a wrong answer never costs diamonds. The PIN-protected parent area sets operations, difficulty, rewards, prices and sounds, with Easy, Medium and Hard presets.

It is designed for children who cannot read yet: colours give the instructions (green means “press to go on”) and the main buttons say their names aloud. No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking; it works fully offline and progress syncs only through the user's own iCloud account.

Key features

  • Free; no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics
  • 82 items in 13 categories; fitting room, basket and search
  • Girl and boy characters; name, skin, eye colour, expression and hair colour are free
  • 5 scenes and a pet; save outfits and a photo album
  • Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division; questions read aloud
  • Daily gift and streak; wrong answers never cost diamonds
  • PIN-protected parent settings: difficulty, rewards, prices, sound, statistics
  • Fully offline; optional iCloud sync across devices
  • Turkish and English; VoiceOver and Reduce Motion support
