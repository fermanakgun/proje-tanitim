Snapage — Support

Türkçe

How do I scan?

Tap the scan button on the home screen, choose a mode (Document, Invoice, ID) and frame the page. Edges are detected automatically; you can crop, rotate and reorder pages and save as PDF.

What are the free limits?

The free version allows 3 new scans per week and up to 12 per month, with up to 3 pages per scan. Your existing documents can always be opened and shared.

What does Pro unlock?

Snapage Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription; Family Sharing is on). It unlocks unlimited scanning, OCR (searchable PDF, TXT, DOCX), PDF tools (merge, split, compress, sign, fill forms), the converter and invoice table export (CSV/XLSX). You can try each Pro feature 3 times before buying.

Restoring a purchase

On a new device or after reinstalling, use “Restore Purchase” in Settings. For a refund, use Apple’s report-a-problem page (reportaproblem.apple.com); Apple processes the payment.

An invoice field was read wrong

On the invoice detail screen you can correct the date, company, amount, VAT and invoice number by hand. If a Turkish e-invoice/e-archive QR code is present, values are read from the QR.

Where is my data?

Only on your device; Snapage makes no network connections and the developer cannot access your data. See the privacy policy.
