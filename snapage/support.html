Snapage — Support

Türkçe

How do I scan?

Tap the Scan button on the home screen, choose a source (camera, photos, files) and a mode (Document, Invoice, ID) and frame the page. Edges are detected automatically and the page is captured automatically once it is steady. You can crop, rotate and reorder pages and save as PDF. If you prefer the system document scanner over Snapage’s own camera screen, turn on “Use system document scanner” in the scan defaults in Settings.

Is Snapage paid? Are there limits?

No. Snapage is completely free and ad-free; there are no scan limits, no subscription and no locked features. The in-app tip is optional and unlocks nothing. To request a refund for a tip, use Apple’s report-a-problem page (reportaproblem.apple.com); Apple processes the payment.

How does iCloud sync work?

If iCloud is enabled for Snapage on iPhones and iPads signed in with the same Apple Account, your documents sync automatically. If they do not, check that the device is online, you are signed in to iCloud and your iCloud storage is not full. Your data lives only in your own private iCloud database.

How do I use the share extension and the widget?

In another app, pick a document or image and choose Snapage in the Share menu; the file is imported into Snapage. For the widget, touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen and add the Snapage widget; you can also add the “Scan” button to Control Center.

App lock and language

You can turn on app lock with Face ID / device passcode in Settings; there is no separate Snapage password. You can also choose the app language in Settings (22 languages; the change applies after you reopen the app).

An invoice field was read wrong

On the invoice detail screen you can correct the date, company, amount, VAT and invoice number by hand. If a Turkish e-invoice/e-archive QR code is present, values are read from the QR.

Where is my data?

On your device and, if you use iCloud sync, in your own iCloud account; the developer cannot access your data. See the privacy policy.
