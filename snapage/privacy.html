Snapage — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18 · Türkçe

Personal data

Snapage does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account or sign-up.

Where your documents stay

Your scans, PDFs, text recognition (OCR) results and the search index are stored only on your device. The developer cannot access them.

Network

Snapage makes no network connections; your documents are never sent to any server. Scanning, invoice field reading, text recognition, PDF tools and conversion all happen on your device.

Ads, analytics and tracking

The app has no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.

Camera and photos

Camera access is requested only when you want to scan a document, and photo access only when you want to create a document from photos. Images never leave your device.

Sharing

Sharing a document is your choice; it uses the iOS Share menu and where you send it is under your control.

Purchase

Snapage Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple; no payment information reaches the developer.
