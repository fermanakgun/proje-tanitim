Snapage — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Türkçe

Personal data

Snapage does not collect, store or share any personal data (App Store label: “Data Not Collected”). No account or sign-up is needed. The developer has no server, account system, ads, analytics or tracking.

Where your documents stay

Your scans, PDFs, text recognition (OCR) results and the search index are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud sync, only in your own iCloud account. The developer cannot access this data.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync keeps your documents up to date across your iPhone and iPad signed in with the same Apple Account. Data is written to your private database in Apple’s CloudKit; only you can access that database, and the developer can neither access nor see it. Sync is an Apple service and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. If you turn iCloud off on your device or delete a document, sync for it stops.

Network

Snapage does not connect to any server owned by the developer or any third party; your documents are never sent to a server. Scanning, invoice field reading, text recognition, PDF tools and conversion all happen on your device. Network traffic occurs only for optional iCloud sync and tip payments, through the system’s (Apple’s) services.

Ads, analytics and tracking

The app has no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.

Camera, photos and Face ID

Camera access is requested only when you want to scan a document, and photo access only when you want to create a document from photos. Images never leave your device. If you turn on app lock, iOS performs the check (Face ID or device passcode); biometric data never reaches the app.

Share extension, widget and sharing

The share extension only hands a document or image you pick in another app to Snapage on your device; files do not leave the device. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widget reads information from your library that is shown only on your device. Sharing a document is your choice; it uses the iOS Share menu and where you send it is under your control.

Tip payments

Snapage is completely free and every feature is available to everyone. An optional in-app tip (through Apple’s in-app purchase system) unlocks nothing. Payment is processed entirely by Apple; no personal data or payment details reach the developer.
