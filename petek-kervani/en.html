About

Comb Couriers is a puzzle where bees peel a pixel picture from the outside in. Pick a box from the box grid and it slides into a launch pad; its bees circle the edge of the picture and carry squares of their own colour to the hive.

The game is currently in development and is not on the App Store yet.

Highlights

  • A box grid and 5 launch pads: you choose the order; if every pad is stuck and no move is left, you are locked
  • Bees circle the edge of the picture and take only squares open to the outside: it peels from the outside in
  • Linked, key-and-lock and hidden boxes keep every level fresh
  • Jokers: Extra Pad, Two Boxes, Recall, Swap; win without jokers to earn the Pure Honey badge
  • One endless path: it starts with 30 handcrafted levels and never ends; if you struggle, the game gently eases up
  • Honey Jar: it fills as you progress, and joker offers are ad-free
  • iPhone and iPad, Turkish and English
  • Fully offline, no ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no personal data collected
